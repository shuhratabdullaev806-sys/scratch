## Working preferences

- In Uzbek video scripts, Veo prompts and captions, write accounting entries as "Debet 50 10 Kredit 46 10": never Dt/Kt, and put a space inside account numbers (50 10, not 5010).
- Never use abbreviations in spoken or on-screen Uzbek text (write "Mas’uliyati cheklangan jamiyat", not MChJ), because the Veo narrator mispronounces them.
