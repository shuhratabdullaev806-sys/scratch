🔒 Davrni Yopish (Zakritiya Perioda)

Ushbu funksiya barcha daromad (9000, 9300, 9500) va xarajat (9100, 9400, 9600, 9800) schyotlarini yopib, Yakuniy Moliyaviy Natijani (9910) aniqlaydi (BHMS 21).

Yopiladigan Schyotlar:
  • Daromadlar (kredit qoldiq): 9010, 9020, 9030, 9310, 9390, 9510, 9520, 9540 ... -> Dt schyot, Kt 9910
  • Xarajatlar (debet qoldiq): 9040, 9110, 9410, 9420, 9430, 9610, 9620 ... -> Dt 9910, Kt schyot
  • 9200, 9700 schyotlari ham qoldig'iga qarab 9910 ga yopiladi
  • Foyda Solig'i: avval qo'lda Dt 9810 Kt 6410 kiritiladi, so'ng 9810 -> 9910 (Dt 9910, Kt 9810)
  • Sof Foyda: Dt 9910, Kt 8710 (Taqsimlanmagan foyda); Zarar: Dt 8710, Kt 9910
